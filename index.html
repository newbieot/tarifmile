How to prepare a tariff workbook

Follow the four-stage workflow without leaving this page.

  1. Set Customer DetailsEnter the customer ID, Salesforce number, and effective period.
  2. Import Tariff RoutesUse XLSX, XLS, or CSV with RUTE KANTOR, TARIF, and Layanan headers.
  3. Review and ValidateResolve incomplete, invalid, and duplicate route-service combinations.
  4. Export WorkbookConfirm the summary and download the TariffCustomer worksheet.
Route formatUse ORIGIN|DESTINATION, for example 29400|10110.
